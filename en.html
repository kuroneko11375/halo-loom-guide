What’s new in v0.6.0

A new Halloween theme plus a full refresh of the light effects, sounds and menu. Every image is an actual Unity render. (Image captions are in Japanese.)

Halloween theme

Slime flows through the bubble ring and a witch’s sigil opens as you charge. The released ghost bursts into bats when it hits something.

Radiance layer

Rebuilt from the ground up: a charge sigil, ready ripples, then a flash, light lances, comets and light feathers.

Bubble rings

A smoother rounded heart and a clearer circle ring, with liquid pockets flowing one by one.

Heart starburst

Launches straight up from the ring center and fills the sky with colorful hearts.

Starfield ring

Six original constellations are drawn line by line across the night sky.

Three fire modes

Standard ornaments fly forward from your fist as Scatter, Beam or Volley.

  • New sounds: the charge sound now runs all the way to full charge, and rings that launch into the sky get a twinkling star sound.
  • Tidier menu: everything lives in a single “Halo Loom” menu, the most-used controls sit on the first page, and every item has an icon.
  • Idle sparkle: turn the ever-drifting stardust and glow on or off from the menu at any time.
  • Cleaner tool window: “Base effect” now comes first, and the special rings have their own section.
  • Fixes: Apply Changes now updates the mesh when you change shape, and the extra circle around heart halos is gone.

Important: to update, remove first and reinstall

  1. Back up your project
  2. Use the old tool’s Remove / Restore to remove the Halo you want to update
  3. Save the scene, then delete only the old HaloMaker tool files
  4. Import the new .unitypackage
  5. Create the Halo again and save the scene

Do not delete: avatars, other Halos, VRChat SDK / Modular Avatar / NDMF, external assets, Generated assets you cannot confirm you own, or .HaloLoomArchive (used for restoring). This is not a “delete the whole HaloMaker folder” procedure.

Note down or screenshot your current settings (colors, ornaments, motion) so recreating the Halo is easy.

Finally, run Build & Test on your avatar and try the controls in VRChat.

01Getting started

Tested with Unity 2022.3.22f1 · PC · Built-in Render Pipeline · VRChat Avatars SDK 3.10.5 · Modular Avatar 1.18.7 · NDMF 1.14.8 (install SDK, MA and NDMF separately). Quest, mobile and URP are not covered.

  1. Import and open

    Import the whole .unitypackage and open 黒猫Dev › Halo Loom from the menu bar.

  2. Choose your avatar

    Drag your avatar from the Hierarchy into the Avatar field. For a Humanoid avatar the Head becomes the mount point; change “Attach To” for wrists, back and so on.

  3. Pick a look

    At the top of the Shape & look tab, choose a standard effect or a special ring under “Base effect” (see the next chapter).

  4. Size and motion

    Adjust the radius, then pick a motion in the Motion tab.

  5. Create and preview

    Press Create Halo and check the preview. After later edits, press Apply Changes.

  6. Save and test in VRChat

    Save the scene and use the SDK’s Build & Test to hold, charge and release it for real.

The free edition opens from 黒猫Dev › Halo Loom Free. The flow is the same with fewer looks and motions.

02The tool window

The window has four tabs. If in doubt, start at the top of Shape & look and work downward.

Shape & look
Base effect → Halo style → Display → Radius → Color, in that order.
Motion
Motion type, loop time and direction, plus the hold / return (grab) settings.
Effects
In-VRChat effects (charge time, Radiance layer, idle-sparkle default) and the effect preview.
Advanced
Random seeds, bake quality and other details. You can leave these alone at first.

Base effect: standard effects and special rings

Standard effects

Pick Particle Arc, Energy Line or Bubble Cluster, then combine a Halo style (circle, heart, flower) with a Display mode (effects only, ornaments only, or both). Shots fly forward from your fist in three modes.

Special rings

Circle Bubble Ring, Heart Bubble Ring and Starfield Ring. Each ring has a fixed shape and its own release show that launches upward from the ring center. Display is “Ring only” or “Ring + ornaments”.

The Halloween theme is available for the Circle and Heart Bubble Rings only.

03Looks and motion

20 motions

Choose from five categories: floating, spirals, hops, formations and showpieces. “Random A / B / C” chains three compatible motions in order (the combination is saved; it is not re-rolled every loop). Collision-style motions are visual only — there is no physics.

Starfield ring

Tiny stars fill a hollow 3D ring; adjust twinkle, short flashes, drift and trails. The starfield runs its own animation, and any ornaments you add use the normal motions.

20 built-in ornaments

Stars, crystals, hearts, feathers and more — 20 built-in ornaments with adjustable count, layout, orientation, size, color and glow. You can also bring your own (see “Custom assets and mounting”).

The preview plays what has been applied. After changing settings, Apply Changes and then preview. Use “Duplicate” to keep alternate versions.

04Hold, charge, release, return

In VRChat you handle the halo with your own hands (either hand works).

  1. Hold

    Touch the halo, then make a fist to take it. It stays in that hand even if you open it.

  2. Charge

    Keep a thumbs-up with the holding hand for the charge time (3 s by default, 2–4 s). A sigil opens and the charge sound plays until full. Changing the gesture early cancels.

  3. Release

    After the ready cue, make a fist away from the original spot on your head to fire. The halo stays in your hand, so you can charge again.

  4. Return

    Bring the holding hand back to the original spot on your head, open it, then make a fist again. Returning always wins, even when fully charged.

  5. Switch hands

    Open the holding hand, touch the halo with the other hand and make a fist (charging restarts).

Which way it fires

→

Standard-effect ornaments

Forward from your fist (wrist toward knuckles), following your wrist.

↑

Special rings

Straight up from the ring center, unfolding overhead.

↗

Halloween ghost

The ring effects go straight up while the ghost alone flies forward from your fist and bursts into bats when it hits a wall or a player (it never covers anyone’s screen).

Holding a left-wrist Halo with the left hand (or right with right) keeps touching the home spot, so returning may win over firing. Use the other hand, or a mount point that does not move with the holding hand.

PC: Halo Loom › PC › “Expand halo” → turn “ON: Charge / OFF: Fire” on → turn it off after full charge to fire. Turning it off early cancels.

If nothing happens, unlock gestures in VRChat and check that your hand actually switches to thumbs-up / fist.

05In-game menu

Only one entry, “Halo Loom”, is added to your avatar menu. Inside, controls are ordered by how often you use them.

Halo Loom
  • Fire mode ›Scatter / Beam / Volley (standard effects only; hidden for special rings)
  • Show haloShow or hide the whole halo
  • Interaction effectsTurn the hold / charge / release effects on or off
  • Look ›Hue, intensity, motion trails and idle sparkle
  • Reset effectsReset the effects
  • Cancel & returnStop at any time and return the halo
  • PC ›Desktop expand, charge / fire and cancel

“Show halo” and “Interaction effects” appear when enabled in the tool. The idle-sparkle default is set in the tool’s Effects tab. Menu labels follow the language you chose in the tool.

06Custom assets and mounting

  1. Set Display to “Ornaments” or “Effects + Ornaments”.

  2. Set the asset type to custom and assign a Prefab from the Project window (or a Mesh and Material). Hierarchy objects cannot be used directly.

  3. Adjust count, layout and size, then set orientation (along path / outward / toward center, etc.) and rotation offset.

  4. Press Apply Changes. Your original Prefab, Mesh and Material are never modified; generated copies are used.

Not just the head

Change “Attach To” to a wrist, chest, back or any Transform, then adjust position, rotation and radius for that spot (there is no automatic fitting).

More assets means more load — start with a small count. VRChat compatibility of third-party assets is not guaranteed. A Generic AnimationClip can drive the asset’s own animation (Humanoid clips and animation events are not supported).

08Troubleshooting

Shader not found / everything is pink

Re-import the whole package (Editor, Shaders, Visuals, Audio). The full version lives in Assets/黒猫Dev/HaloMaker, the free one in Assets/黒猫Dev/HaloLoomFree.

My change doesn’t show up

Press Apply Changes — with “Apply changes live” off, editing a field alone does not update the generated halo. Also check that “Editing Halo” at the top is the one you mean.

I see a circle or light band around the halo

In the Scene view animations are not running, so parts that are hidden in game can show. Also check that a duplicated avatar is not overlapping.

Hand controls don’t respond

Unlock gestures, touch the halo with your own hand, then make a fist. After edits: Apply Changes → save the scene → Build & Test.

It feels heavy

Lower the ornament count or star density and test with lighter assets. Turning off idle sparkle also helps.

It looks different elsewhere

Images are Unity Editor (Built-in) renders. World lighting and overlapping transparency change how it looks.

Still stuck? Contact us from the BOOTH page with your version, Unity version, steps to reproduce and any Console errors. BOOTH↗
