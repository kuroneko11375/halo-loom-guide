01Get started

Back up your Unity project before editing. This guide is based on Unity 2022.3 and the Built-in Render Pipeline. Check the BOOTH listing for current requirements and terms.

Important: import the whole product folder, including both Editor and Shaders. Do not move the shaders into the Editor folder.

  1. Import and open

    Extract the downloaded ZIP and import its .unitypackage into Unity. Once compilation finishes, choose 黒猫Dev > Halo Loom from the top menu.

  2. Assign your Avatar and attachment

    In Basic, drag the scene Avatar root from the Hierarchy into Avatar. A valid Humanoid initially uses Head. If Head cannot be resolved, assign the attachment Transform yourself.

  3. Set position and size

    Adjust Position, Rotation and Radius, or enable Adjust in Scene View. Start with a small radius and check for overlap with the head and ears.

  4. Choose the appearance

    In Appearance, choose base effects, ornaments, or a mix of both. Adjust color, count and size. For your own models, see Custom assets & attachment below.

  5. Choose the motion

    In Motion, select a category and preset, then adjust loop duration. A starfield-only halo instead uses twinkle, movement and trails.

  6. Create and preview

    Click Create Halo, then Preview Motion. When editing an existing halo, apply your changes before previewing.

  7. Save and check in use

    Save your Unity scene. For VRChat avatars, also check with the SDK’s Build & Test. Keep the Generated assets referenced by any halo still in use.

Using Free? Open 黒猫Dev > Halo Loom Free instead. The basic workflow is similar, but the available effects and motions differ.

03Custom assets & attachment

Pro supports your own small props as well as stars and crystals. Make something elegant, playful, or a little unexpected.

  1. In Appearance, choose Ornaments or a mix of effects and ornaments.
  2. Select Custom as the asset source and assign a Prefab from the Project window, not a scene instance from the Hierarchy. You can use Mesh + Material instead.
  3. Set count, placement and scale. Radius changes the orbit; ornament scale changes the asset itself.
  4. Choose Follow Path, Face Outward, Face Center or another orientation. Adjust the rotation offset when the model’s own forward axis does not match.
  5. Choose motion, then create or apply. Generated instances are used; the original Prefab, Mesh and Material assets are not edited.

Not just above the head

In Basic, change the attachment to a wrist, chest, back Transform or your own anchor. Adjust local position, rotation and radius to suit the location. This is manual placement, not automatic fitting to each body part.

More copies mean more polygons, renderers and potentially particles. Start with a low count. Cost estimates do not capture every runtime effect or transparent overlap. Halo Loom does not guarantee compatibility for third-party assets or runtime components.
Can the asset play its own animation?

An optional Generic AnimationClip can animate the asset independently of the outer halo motion. Bindings must be relative to the asset root. Humanoid clips, Animation Events and mismatched bindings are not supported. If the Prefab already has an Animator Controller or legacy Animation, leave the extra clip empty to preserve that setup, or provide an asset without a controller. Editor preview of third-party Animator setups is not guaranteed.

04Effects & motion

These are actual Unity examples, not additional bundled one-click presets. Energy lines have adjustable width; bubbles have a transparency control.

20 motions for ornaments and light bodies

Choose from gentle, spiral, hopping, formation and performance categories. Fit your assets with a calm motion first, then try a toroidal helix, hops or a collapse sequence. Clash motions are visual choreography, not physics collisions. Large assets may overlap.

A starfield with twinkle and small movements

Tiny four-point stars fill a hollow, three-dimensional ring. Appearance controls density, star size and ring thickness; Motion controls twinkle, flashes, movement and trails. The starfield has its own animation, while ornaments in a mixed style can use regular motion presets.

Random A / B / C

Select at least three compatible candidates to bake three different motions into an A → B → C loop. Loop duration covers all three sections. The selection is saved, not freshly randomized every cycle at runtime. Use the reshuffle control for a new combination.

Preview plays applied animation. Apply after changing settings. Before editing, check the current Halo name at the top of the window. Use Create Copy when you want to keep the existing version.

06Troubleshooting

Shader missing, or everything is pink

Check that both Editor and Shaders were imported, then reimport the complete package. Pro lives in Assets/黒猫Dev/HaloMaker; Free in Assets/黒猫Dev/HaloLoomFree. Shaders must stay outside Editor. If it persists, include the shader error from Console and your environment when contacting support.

An existing halo is not detected

Select the actual Halo object in the Hierarchy and check the editing target at the top. Select halos individually when there is more than one. Avoid creating another halo until you have checked selection and Avatar assignment. A read-only legacy object can be kept while you configure a new halo.

Preview does not reflect my changes

Apply the changes first. If live apply is off, changing a field alone does not update the generated halo. Also check which halo is being edited.

Grabbing or sway does not work

Enable Grab & Gentle Sway in Pro’s Basic page, then apply. It requires the VRChat SDK. Normal animation preview does not simulate PhysBone grabbing. Check interaction permissions and behavior in VRChat. This moves the halo as a whole; it does not deform the ring into a soft loop.

The effect is expensive or too crowded

Reduce ornament count or star density and test with a lightweight asset. Check particles, multiple renderers and transparent overlap in custom Prefabs. A cost warning is not a complete performance diagnosis or FPS guarantee.

What should I keep when updating or deleting?

Back up the project before updating. Keep Generated controllers and their sub-assets while halos depend on them. Do not bulk-delete generated assets if you are unsure what uses them. Keep your source assets separately.

Will it look the same everywhere?

Images were captured in Unity Editor / Built-in / DX11. World lighting, cameras and transparent overlap can change the result. This does not establish support for every platform, including Quest, mobile or URP. Check your target avatar in its actual environment.
